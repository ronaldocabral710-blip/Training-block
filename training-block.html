Workouts by Type

Three options per run type. HR and pace targets apply to the working portion — warm-ups and cooldowns are always easy (Z1).

Easy / Recovery

<156 bpm · 9:00–9:45/mi
01
Standard Recovery
30 min easy, flat route. Keep HR under 156 — don't chase pace.
02
Shakeout
20 min very easy the day after a hard session. Walk breaks fine if HR creeps up.
03
Long Easy
60–75 min at easy pace. Slight HR drift late in the run is expected and fine.

Aerobic / Base

02

50 min: first 30 min easy (Z1), last 20 min nudge into Z2.
03
Long Aerobic
70–90 min, majority Z1, final 15–20 min controlled into Z2.

Progression rate: weekly increase averages ~13–15%, slightly above the common "10% rule" — reasonable across a 4-week block, but scale back mid-block if recovery markers (resting HR, sleep, motivation) are trending the wrong way.
Quality build pattern: pace and HR targets stay fixed all 4 weeks. Progress comes from accumulating more reps/time in each zone (3→4→4→5 threshold reps, 4→5→6→6 VO2max reps) — never from pushing paces faster.
After this block: no down week is built in here. If continuing past Week 4, drop back to ~32–35 mi in Week 5 before building again.
